# Anna University M.E. Project Report: Format Guide

**Project:** Auditable AI Agents  
**Prepared:** 2026-10-30  
**Read this first:** I could not find one official, current Anna University M.E. report manual online. This guide is built from the two most authoritative documents I could open and read in full:

1. **Anna University, Centre for Distance Education:** "Guidelines for Preparation of MCA Project Report (Prescribed Format and Specification)", an official Anna University document ([cde.annauniv.edu/pdf/MCA/report.pdf](https://cde.annauniv.edu/pdf/MCA/report.pdf)). It is for MCA, but the layout rules are the university's standard ones.
2. **Panimalar Engineering College:** "Guidelines for Preparation of M.E./M.Tech Phase I Report and Phase II Thesis", an Anna-affiliated college's manual written specifically for M.E. ([Panimalar-PG-Thesis-Format.pdf](https://mail.coe.panimalar.ac.in/downloads/Panimalar-PG-Thesis-Format.pdf)).

The two agree on almost every layout rule; where they differ I say so. **Your own department's manual always wins.** Take this guide to your project coordinator and confirm the rows marked "confirm".

---

## 1. Report types

| Report | When | Minimum size (Chapter 1 to last appendix page) |
|---|---|---|
| Phase I report | First project phase | 25 pages |
| Phase II thesis | Final project phase | 40 pages, with more weight on results and discussion |

The official MCA guideline instead caps its report at 60 pages; confirm whether your department sets a minimum, a maximum or both.

## 3. Page, margins, paper

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Paper | A4 (297 x 210 mm); bound size 290 x 205 mm |
| Top margin | 30 to 35 mm |
| Bottom margin | 25 to 30 mm |
| Left margin | 35 to 40 mm (binding side) |
| Right margin | 20 to 25 mm |
| Paper quality | Good white paper, not lighter than 80 gsm |
| Ink | Black |
| Tables and figures | Must fit inside the margins |

---

## 4. Typing

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Font | Times New Roman |
| General text size | **13 pt** (Panimalar M.E. manual). The official MCA guideline uses 12 pt for certificate and abstract. **Confirm 12 or 13.** |
| General text spacing | 1.5 lines |
| Single spacing | Long tables, long quotations, footnotes, multi-line captions, references |
| Double spacing | Bonafide certificate, acknowledgement, abstract |
| Quotations over one line | Indented 15 mm from both margins |
| Corrections | Not permitted in submitted copies |
| Sub-heading at page bottom | Needs at least two lines below it, else move to next page |
| Hyphenation | Do not split the last word of a page with a hyphen |

---

## 5. Headings and page layout

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Headings of items 2 to 10 | CAPITALS, no punctuation, centred, 50 mm below top of page; text starts four spaces below |
| Chapter opening | The word CHAPTER (no punctuation) centred 50 mm from the top; two spaces below, the chapter title in capitals, centred; text starts four spaces below the title |
| Division and sub-division headings | Left-justified, with number, starting 20 mm from the left margin |
| Paragraphs | First letter offset 20 mm from the left margin; paragraphs start three spaces below the previous one |
| Title and chapter headings | Same format as in the table of contents |

---

## 6. Numbering

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Page numbers | No punctuation, **top right corner**, 20 mm from the top, last digit aligned with the right margin |
| Preliminary pages (items 1 to 8) | Lower-case Roman numerals; the title page counts as (i) but the number is not printed |
| Main text | Arabic numerals starting at Chapter 1, page 1 |
| Chapters, divisions | Decimal: division 4 under division 3 of chapter 2 is **2.3.4** |
| Tables and figures | By chapter: the 4th figure in chapter 3 is **Figure 3.4**; tables likewise |
| Appendix figures | Figure 3 in Appendix 2 is **Figure A 2.3** |
| Equations | Restart each chapter: the 8th equation of chapter 2 is **(2.8)**, cited as Equation (2.8) |
| Appendices | Appendix 1, Appendix 2 and so on |

---

## 7. Tables, figures, references

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Table caption | **Above** the table, **left-aligned**, sentence case ("Table 3.1 Test results") |
| Figure caption | **Below** the figure, **centred**, sentence case |
| Placement | Near where first cited; on the same page if half a page or less, separated from text by triple spacing |
| Continued table | Caption "Table 2.1 (continued)", centred and underlined; no line under an unfinished table |
| In-text citation | Author-year: (Tsychiya 1980), Jankins and Walts (1968), Shin et al. (1984) |
| Reference list | Heading REFERENCES; alphabetical by first author; single spacing, left-justified |
| Reference entry | Authors (year), 'Title', Journal, Vol., No., pp. |
| Your own publications | Not in the references; list them in an appendix titled List of Publications |

> The Panimalar manual uses the author-year style above, not IEEE numbers. Your IEEE conference paper (see `docs/thesis/ieee-abstract.md`) still uses IEEE numbering; the report follows the college style. **Confirm** that your department accepts author-year.

## 8. Specimen pages

**Title page** (Times New Roman, bold; all centred):

| Line | Size |
|---|---|
| TITLE OF THESIS | 18 pt, 1.5 spacing |
| A THESIS (or PHASE I REPORT) | 14 pt |
| *Submitted by* | 14 pt, italic |
| NAME OF THE CANDIDATE | 16 pt |
| in partial fulfillment for the award of the degree of | 14 pt, 1.5 spacing |
| MASTER OF ENGINEERING IN [PROGRAMME] | 16 pt |
| DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING | 16 pt, 1.5 spacing |
| COLLEGE NAME, (affiliation line) | 16 pt / 12 pt |
| MONTH AND YEAR | 14 pt |

**Bonafide certificate:** college name 18 pt, affiliation line 12 pt, BONAFIDE CERTIFICATE 16 pt, body 13 pt. Wording: *Certified that this [Report/Thesis] titled "TITLE" is the bonafide work of NAME (Register No.) who carried out the work under my supervision. Certified further that to the best of my knowledge the work reported herein does not form part of any other thesis or dissertation on the basis of which a degree or award was conferred on an earlier occasion on this or any other candidate.* Two signature blocks: the supervisor (name, academic designation, department, address) and the Head of Department.

**Table of contents:** heading 14 pt; columns CHAPTER NO., TITLE, PAGE NO.; the abstract, lists and chapters listed with their page numbers (roman for the front matter, Arabic for chapters).
